## Status

These files preserve outputs of the retired pipeline. They have not been regenerated or verified against the corrected implementation. Labels such as "Professional Astroturfer", "Highly Partisan", and "Targets" in the original images are unsupported interpretations, not established facts about users.

The original prose described an 80% consistency threshold while the charts show 68%; neither implies statistical confidence. The classifier had no minimum matched-comment requirement, used ambiguous keywords, and relied on unvalidated behavioral flags. Do not cite these charts as current findings. Reproduce corrected analyses using the [root instructions](../../README.md) and retain their generated manifests in separate run directories.
